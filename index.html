Our Core Mission

Standing Together for Humanity

Help Humanity is built on transparency, compassion, and direct action. When crisis hits innocent children and families, we work to get your help to them quickly and show you exactly where it went.

Food & Nutrition

Providing warm meals and clean drinking water to families and young children surviving in extreme crisis zones.

Emergency Medical Aid

Supplying life-saving medicines, first aid kits, and urgent healthcare support to injured children and vulnerable mothers.

Orphan & Child Care

Protecting children, supporting shelter needs, and providing emotional and financial backing in difficult times.

Our Trust Promise

Transparency and Personal Proof

Your trust means everything to us. For every donation you make, we will send you personalized pictures and videos bearing your name, so you can see your contribution reach a person in need.

After donating, email your transaction reference or name to HelpHumanity911@gmail.com. Our team will send your verification media to your inbox.

How we send your proof

  1. 1 Submit your pledge. Choose an amount and enter your email address.
  2. 2 Receive payment details. We will email you the secure payment link directly.
  3. 3 Receive your proof. We send personalized pictures and video clips of the aid distribution bearing your name.
